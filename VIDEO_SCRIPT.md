## Scene Breakdown & Narration

### 🎬 HOOK (0:00–0:03) — 90 frames
**Visual:** Single bee glowing in darkness, particle swarm. Text: "VANISHED"
**Narration:** 
> "What if every bee on Earth... vanished... right now?"

**SFX:** Low drone, wind howl, bee buzz fading to silence
**Music:** Ominous sub-bass drone (55Hz)

### 📅 YEAR 10 (0:50–1:05) — 450 frames
**Visual:** Ocean dead zones expanding. Coastal nurseries gone. Fish stocks crashing. Oxygen dropping.

### 📅 YEAR 50 (1:05–1:35) — 900 frames
**Visual:** Wasteland. Domed cities. Synthetic food. Ruins of civilization. Ancient wind.

**Narration:**

**Music:** Hopeful resolve — major key, building to warm chord

## Visual Style Guide

- **Pixel density:** 120px per voxel (adjustable)
- **Color palette per era:**
  - Healthy: Greens (#2d5a2d, #3a7a3a), floral accents
  - Declining: Yellows/browns (#d4a574, #8b7355)
  - Dead: Desaturated reds (#3d2d1a, #2a1a1a)
  - Void: Dark blues (#0a1525, #050508)
  - Hope: Vibrant greens + gold accents
- **Camera:** Slow orbital, subtle dolly, always purposeful
- **Transitions:** 1-second crossfade with scale shift
- **Text:** Press Start 2P / VT323 pixel fonts
